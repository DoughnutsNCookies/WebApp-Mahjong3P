Effect samples: pick a direction

Each sample is a phone-sized table. Tap 碰 / 槓 / 胡 to fire that moment; the idle motion runs on its own. Effects scale with the event: pong small, kong bigger, hu biggest.

1. Ink and seal 水墨印章

Keeps today's paper look. Calls land as huge brush-written characters on an ink splash; Hu is a giant red chop that slams the screen, then flies into the winner's box. Calm idle: breathing tiles, drifting ink mist.

2. Macau night 金碧輝煌

Green felt, gold trim, swaying red 福 lanterns. Calls are gold characters with spinning light rays; Hu bursts square-holed gold coins (古錢) across the table and the winnings roll up. The most casino.

3. New Year arcade 新年街機

Lunar New Year red and gold, chunky tiles that press down when tapped, a wobbling 福. Calls pop out of a starburst; Hu sets off firecracker confetti. The most playful.
